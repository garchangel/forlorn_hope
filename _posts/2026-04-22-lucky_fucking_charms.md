Night City never stops finding new ways to be absolutely deranged, chooms. Today's flavor of insanity? A gang themed after **Lucky Charms cereal** holding hostages in a hotel called The Shark.

I wish I was making this up.

The crew got tapped by Billy Flight—some fixer for "Choom Goes Room" (still don't know what the fuck that is)—to check out the neighborhood where the Forlorn Hope's getting rebuilt. Simple recon, right? Except an edgerunner team broke into The Shark and locked down floors three and five, leaving floor four crawling with cereal-themed scavs and their unfortunate captives.

Time to go to work.

---

## ❖ Breakfast of Scumbags

The team infiltrates floor four with one goal: extract the hostages, zero casualties. Well, zero *hostage* casualties. The Lucky Charms gang? They're about to learn that magically delicious doesn't mean bulletproof.

**The players in this breakfast-themed bloodbath:**  
• **Pink Heart** - First to find out cereal mascots bleed  
• **Green Clover** - Thinks human shields are a valid tactical choice  
• **Orange Star** - Netrunner coward with zero backbone  
• **Yellow Moon** - Couldn't hit water if they fell out of a boat

Violette jacks in, takes control of the security cameras, and spots Orange Star lurking in the net. Cue the nerd cyber-slapfight in netspace while the crew goes loud.

## ❖ The Real Treasure

After the hostage extraction, the team connects with **Grandma Pierce**, local legend and apparently the only person in Night City with access to actual, legitimate, **fresh vegetables**.

Not synthetic. Not printed. Not irradiated. *Fresh.*

In Night City, that's worth more than the eddies from the job.

---

## ❖ Cereal Killers Get Boxed

Let's break down what went wrong for the Lucky Charms crew:

**Pink Heart:** Thought they were tough. Learned they were pink mist.  
**Green Clover:** Used a hostage as a shield. Got a bullet as a response.  
**Orange Star:** Netrunner with no spine. Violette owned them in the net, Starlight owned them in meatspace.  
**Yellow Moon:** Couldn't aim worth shit. Still ended up dead.

Meanwhile, the crew operated like a precision instrument: controlled entry, coordinated fire, zero hostage casualties, and every scav either flatlined or unconscious.

---

Night City's verdict? If you're gonna theme your gang after breakfast cereal, at least have the skills to back it up. The Lucky Charms crew learned the hard way that marshmallows don't stop bullets, human shields don't stop professionals, and netrunners don't outrun shotguns.

The Shark Hotel's floor four is now scav-free, the hostages are safe, the Forlorn Hope neighborhood just got a whole lot quieter, and Grandma Pierce is handing out vegetables like some kind of post-apocalyptic saint.
