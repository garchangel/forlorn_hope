Just when you think the corpos can't find new ways to endanger civilians, **Biotechnica** drops a fresh nightmare into Woodland Park: **Venus fly trees**.

Yeah. Trees. That eat people.

Because apparently regular trees weren't doing enough for the ecosystem, so some lab-coat genius decided to scale up a houseplant into something that views humans as a snack.

---

## ❖ When Nature Gets an Appetite

Here's how this shitshow unfolded: Biotechnica launches their new "eco-friendly urban solution" (read: weaponized foliage) and plants these botanical horrors in Woodland Park. Marketing probably called them "adaptive vegetation" or some corpo bullshit.

Then some blue-collar workers decide to fuck around near the new trees. 

They found out.

## ❖ Corporate Innovation or Corporate Negligence?

Let's talk about what Biotechnica **didn't** do:

• Put up warning signs  
• Fence off the area  
• Maybe mention that the pretty new trees view humans as prey  
• Consider that blue-collar workers might, I don't know, exist near their murder plants

You know what they **did** do? Plant carnivorous trees in a public park and hoped for the best.

**Biotechnica's innovation checklist:**  
✓ Create man-eating plant  
✓ Deploy in urban environment  
✗ Safety protocols  
✗ Public warning  
✗ Give a single shit about civilian casualties

## ❖ Weird Shit, Even for Night City

Here's the thing, chooms: Night City's got combat zones, cyberpsychos, gangs themed after breakfast cereal, and corpos who treat human life like a quarterly expense. But **man-eating trees in a public park** is a new level of "what the actual fuck."
